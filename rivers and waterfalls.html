Rivers & Waterfalls


Clean Rivers

Rivers are an important part of nature. They provide fresh water for people, animals, and plants. Rivers make the land fertile and help farmers grow crops. Their beauty and peaceful flow attract many visitors.





Amazing Waterfalls

Waterfalls are one of the most beautiful wonders of nature. They create amazing views and a peaceful atmosphere. Many people visit waterfalls to enjoy their beauty and fresh air. Waterfalls make nature more attractive and enjoyable.
