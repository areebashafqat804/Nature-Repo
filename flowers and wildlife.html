Flowers & Wildlife


Beautiful Flowers

Flowers make nature beautiful and colorful. They come in different shapes and colors and make gardens look attractive. Flowers spread a sweet fragrance and bring happiness to people. Roses, lilies, and sunflowers are some of the most beautiful flowers.





Wildlife

Wildlife includes different kinds of animals, birds, and insects that live in nature. They are an important part of our environment and make nature more beautiful. Forests and jungles are home to many wild animals. We should protect wildlife and keep their habitats safe.
