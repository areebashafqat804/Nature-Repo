Mountains & Forests


Beautiful Mountains

Mountains are one of the most beautiful parts of nature. They make the environment peaceful and attractive. Mountains are covered with trees, snow, and green valleys. Many people visit mountains to enjoy the fresh air and beautiful views.





Green Forests

Forests are beautiful places filled with trees, plants, and wildlife. They provide fresh air and help keep our environment clean and healthy. Many birds and animals live in forests. Forests are very important for nature, and we should protect them.
